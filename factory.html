The factory

One site. One team. Fabric in, cartons out.

Lakshmi Manufacturing has produced knitted and light woven apparel at the Athi River Export Processing Zone since February 2015. From a 78,253 sq ft facility outside Nairobi, 18 sewing lines turn out T-shirts, polos, leggings, sportswear and outerwear for major international retail and sporting-goods programs: around 750,000 garments a month, on a weekly vessel schedule out of Mombasa.

The factory is owner-operated. Buyers deal directly with the people who run the floor: one management team covering production, quality, compliance and shipping, all on site. Cutting, sewing, pressing, packing and testing happen under one roof, with C-TPAT-aligned security from the fabric store to container loading.
