PUT THE SITE ONLINE WITH GITHUB — FREE, ~5 MINUTES
(Do this on a computer, not a phone — the upload needs drag-and-drop.)

STEP 1 — MAKE A FREE ACCOUNT
1. Go to  github.com  and click Sign up.
2. Use the factory email. Verify the code they send.

STEP 2 — MAKE A "REPOSITORY" (a folder on GitHub)
1. Click the  +  at the top right  ->  New repository
2. Repository name:  lakshmi-site
3. Leave everything else as it is (Public). Click  Create repository.

STEP 3 — UPLOAD THE WEBSITE
1. On the page that appears, click the small link  "uploading an existing file".
2. Unzip  mahalakshmi-github.zip  on your computer and OPEN the unzipped folder.
3. Select EVERYTHING inside it (all files AND the images folder) and drag it
   into the GitHub upload box. Wait until every file shows a green tick.
4. Click the green  Commit changes  button at the bottom.

STEP 4 — SWITCH THE WEBSITE ON
1. In the repository, click the  Settings  tab (top right).
2. In the left menu click  Pages.
3. Under "Branch" choose  main  and  / (root)  ->  click  Save.
4. Wait about a minute, refresh the page. A box at the top shows your link:

   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/lakshmi-site/

That link works anywhere — WhatsApp it, email it, put it on a business card.

STEP 5 — SWITCH ON THE ENQUIRY FORM (once)
Send a test enquiry from the live site yourself. An email from FormSubmit
arrives at hemantboodia@yahoo.co.uk — open it and click ACTIVATE.
Every enquiry after that lands in the inbox automatically.

LATER — USING mahalakshmi.co.ke INSTEAD
When you're ready: Settings -> Pages -> Custom domain -> type mahalakshmi.co.ke
and press Save, then tell me and I'll give you the exact records to change
at the place the domain was bought.

UPDATING THE SITE
Send me the changes, I update the files, you drag the new files into the
repository the same way (GitHub replaces the old ones).
