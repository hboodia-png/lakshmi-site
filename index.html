The factory

One site. One team. Fabric in, cartons out.

Mahalakshmi Garments has produced knitted and light woven apparel at the Athi River Export Processing Zone since February 2015. From a 78,253 sq ft facility outside Nairobi, 18 sewing lines turn out T-shirts, polos, leggings, sportswear and outerwear for major international retail and sporting-goods programs — around 650,000 garments a month, on a weekly vessel schedule out of Mombasa.

The factory is owner-operated. Buyers deal directly with the people who run the floor: one management team covering production, quality, compliance and shipping, all on site. Cutting, sewing, pressing, packing and testing happen under one roof, with C-TPAT-aligned security from the fabric store to container loading.

Why Kenya

Knitwear into the US normally lands with 16–32% duty. Sewn in Kenya under AGOA, it enters at zero — and the third-country fabric rule keeps that advantage even with fabric milled in Asia.

Quality

Checked at every stage, not just the end.

  1. Fabric inspection

    4-point system, 10% of every dye lot on arrival.

  2. Trims & accessories

    AQL 1.0, Level II sampling before issue to lines.

  3. Cut panel inspection

    Panel checks before bundling reaches the sewing floor.

  4. In-line inspection

    AQL 1.0/1.5 with roving QC — two auditors per line, every line.

  5. End-line & finishing

    100% end-of-line checking plus a finishing audit.

  6. Final inspection

    AQL 1.0/1.5/2.5 to buyer spec, with a 100% accuracy check.

  7. Product safety

    Broken-needle control and 100% metal detection before packing.

On-site testing lab. GSM cutting and weighing, shrinkage testing, pull-strength testing, colour assessment in a UL3500 comparison cabinet with Munsell kit, humidity and lux monitoring — plus 100% metal and needle detection on everything that ships.
