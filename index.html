The factory

One site. One team. Fabric in, cartons out.

Lakshmi Manufacturing has produced knitted and light woven apparel at the Athi River Export Processing Zone since April 2015. From a 93,613 sq ft facility outside Nairobi, 22 sewing lines turn out T-shirts, polos, leggings, sportswear and outerwear for major international retail and sporting-goods programs: around 750,000 garments a month, on a weekly vessel schedule out of Mombasa.

Why Kenya

Duty-free by design.

Knitwear into the US normally lands with 16 to 32% duty. Sewn in Kenya under AGOA, it enters at zero, and the third-country fabric rule keeps that advantage even with fabric milled in Asia.
