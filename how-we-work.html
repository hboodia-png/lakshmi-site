How we work

Enquiry to shipped cartons, step by step.

  1. Enquiry

    Email us your company and country, product type, estimated annual quantity, target delivery date and the tech pack.

  2. Quote

    Within two working days we reply with a price, the MOQ and the lead time for your style, plus any construction or fabric-sourcing notes.

  3. Sampling

    Samples are made on the same lines that will run the bulk and sent to you for fit and construction review.

  4. Approval

    You confirm fit, construction, fabric and trims. Any changes go into a revised sample before bulk.

  5. Purchase order

    On your PO we order fabric and trims. Mills in South East Asia are about 4 weeks by sea from Kenya.

  6. Production

    Cutting, sewing, pressing and packing on 18 lines under one roof, with fabric inspected on arrival and cut panels checked before bundling.

  7. In-line and final QC

    Two roving QC auditors on every line check to AQL 1.0/1.5 during sewing. Final inspection is to AQL 1.0/1.5/2.5 per your spec, and every packed garment passes 100% metal and needle detection.

  8. Shipping

    Containers are loaded on site, video recorded and security checked. Weekly sailings from Mombasa, 4 weeks by sea to the US East Coast; 21 to 25 weeks fabric to US warehouse, all in.
