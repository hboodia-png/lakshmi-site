Responsibility

2,400+ people. 75% women. Run properly.

People

  • ILO core labour standards: no child labour, no forced labour
  • Freedom of association and collective bargaining respected
  • Equal pay across genders
  • 100% accurate, on-time digital wages
  • Safe workplace per Kenya's OSH Act 2007

Environment

  • Rainwater harvesting for domestic use
  • Solar power on security and emergency systems, with a transition plan for production power
  • Environmental Management System in place
  • GRS-certified for recycled materials

Community

  • 1 million face masks distributed locally during Covid-19
  • Drought relief food donations through Kenya Red Cross
  • Seed donations to Kenya's 100,000-tree planting initiative
  • School, orphanage and worker-relief programs
  • Free eye clinics and in-house safety training for workers

Women make up 75% of the workforce. The factory respects maternity rights, provides comfortable seating for expectant mothers, and includes women in its in-house fire safety, first aid and health and safety training.
